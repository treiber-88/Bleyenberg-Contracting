About the studio

A small team that stays close to the work.

Fernwood was founded in 2014 on a simple idea: better design comes from studios small enough that everyone stays close to the work, not layered behind account managers and decks.

How we started

Fernwood began as a two-person shop working out of a shared studio space, taking on brand and product work for early-stage teams who couldn't yet afford a large agency. That constraint shaped how we still work today: lean teams, direct communication, and no unnecessary layers between the client and the people doing the work.

Over a decade later, we've grown to nine people and moved into our own studio, but the way we take on projects hasn't changed much at all.

How we work

Every engagement starts with research before anything gets drawn — talking to users, auditing what exists, and understanding the constraints a client is actually working within. Design decisions are easier to defend when they're grounded in something real.

We keep project teams small, usually two to three people, so the same hands that sketch the first concept are still there refining the final detail.

Who we work with

Most of our clients are founders and product leads at early- and growth-stage companies, though we also take on select brand work for established organizations that are rethinking how they show up.

What guides the work

  • Clarity over decoration

    If a detail doesn't help someone understand or use the thing, it doesn't belong.

  • Research before opinion

    We'd rather be slow and right than fast and confident about the wrong problem.

  • Craft at every scale

    The same care goes into a button state as into the overall system it lives in.
